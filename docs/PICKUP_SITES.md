# HarbourCart pickup-site shortlist

Research pass: **2026-09-28**

This is a location screen, not proof that any venue permits recurring commercial grocery pickup. Every candidate requires written venue permission and must remain compatible with the Nova Scotia food-safety classification.

## 1. Bus Stop Theatre Co-op — Community Room

- 2203 Gottingen Street, Halifax
- published non-member rate: **$60 for up to 4 hours + HST**
- full day: $100 for 8 hours + HST
- room capacity: 50
- source: https://rentals.busstoptheatre.coop/
- rental terms: https://rentals.busstoptheatre.coop/terms-conditions/

### Why it is interesting

At the current four-hour pickup model this reduces the site-price screen from the earlier conservative $160/week to $60/week.

Combined with:
- 25 km local route × $0.73/km = $18.25
- pilot software screen ≈ $23.12/week

known modelled weekly cost becomes roughly **$101.37** before HST, insurance, fixed admin, food-use/site extras and other unresolved costs.

At the working ~$7.28 variable contribution per household, that is about **14 households** to cover these known modelled costs.

### Gate

The published rental terms do not establish that HarbourCart may use the Community Room for recurring commercial grocery handoff or temporary food holding. Obtain written permission before using this as an operating assumption.

## 2. Halifax Brewery Market — event rental / market relationship

- 1496 Lower Water Street, Halifax
- year-round Saturday farmers market
- event rental sheet lists:
  - Pu'Taliewey Room: **$60/hour standard**
  - Lower Courtyard: $75/hour standard
  - Upper Courtyard: $100/hour standard
  - setup/tear-down billed at 50% of hourly rate
- source: https://www.halifaxbrewerymarket.com/
- current rental sheet is linked from the market site

The market explicitly supports local-food producers and says it is open to interesting ideas, which makes a partnership conversation worthwhile. The published event-rental rate itself is not the cheapest option.

### Seasonal alternate

The affiliated Novalea Farmers Market publishes a **$35 table fee** for a 10 ft canopy/tables during its seasonal market. That is a vendor relationship, not a generic pickup-site rental, and requires compliance with market rules and applicable food regulations.

## 3. Halifax Forum Farmers' Market

- 6205 Almon Street, Halifax
- year-round Saturdays 8am–1pm
- official site actively accepts applications from farm/food vendors
- free parking / free customer admission
- source: https://forumfarmersmarket.ca/

No current official vendor fee was found in the public material reviewed. Request current terms rather than relying on older community reports.

## 4. HRM recreation/community rooms

HRM supports online room rentals across municipal recreation centres. Current public room-rate schedules remain useful for screening, but a room booking does not itself establish that recurring food pickup is permitted.

Potential central facilities to ask:
- George Dixon Centre
- Citadel Community Centre
- Halifax Central Common Multi-Purpose Room
- St. Andrew's Community Centre

Source / booking guidance:
https://www.halifax.ca/parks-recreation/programs-activities/bookings-rentals

## Decision criteria

The preferred pilot site should optimize:

1. written permission for HarbourCart's exact activity;
2. compatibility with regulator requirements;
3. total weekly site cost;
4. easy Saturday/evening access;
5. tables/loading access;
6. customer transit/walkability;
7. parking for pickup;
8. secure short-term holding;
9. no long-term lease;
10. ability to repeat weekly.

A cheaper room that cannot legally/contractually support the food workflow is not a viable site.

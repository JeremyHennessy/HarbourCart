# HarbourCart supplier quote request pack

Prepared: **2026-09-28**

These are ready-to-send research requests. They are not evidence that contact has occurred or that any supplier has quoted HarbourCart.

## Standard commercial terms requested

Every response should capture:

- exact product / variety / grade;
- pack or case quantity;
- supplier-packed consumer format if available;
- minimum order;
- unit/case price;
- delivery to central Halifax or supplier-pickup terms;
- delivery minimum/fee;
- price validity;
- substitution policy;
- payment terms;
- marketable seconds / B-grade option quoted separately;
- whether committed higher volume unlocks another tier.

HarbourCart should retain the original email/PDF/quote before marking any offer `VERIFIED_QUOTE`.
